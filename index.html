ThreeTasks

Stop deciding. Start doing.

How it works

In the Tasks tab, add tasks to your Morning, Daytime and Evening lists. Each day, shake your iPhone (or tap the button) and ThreeTasks picks one task from each list. Tick them off as you go. Tomorrow you get three fresh picks.

Because every task lives in one list, a morning task like “have a coffee and read” will never be picked for your evening.

When you add a task, choose whether it's one-off or recurring. A one-off task leaves your list once it's picked. If you don't tick it off that day, it goes back in the list. A recurring task stays in the list and can be picked again on future days.

FAQ

Shaking doesn't do anything.
A shake fills any of today's slots that are still empty. If all three are already picked, there's nothing left to pick until tomorrow. If a list is empty, add a task to it first.

Can I re-roll a task I don't like?
No, and that's the point. Letting the app decide is what gets you started.

Are my tasks backed up?
Tasks are stored only on your device, so they're included in your normal iPhone backup but aren't synced elsewhere.
