Privacy Policy

ThreeTasks · Last updated 5 November 2026

ThreeTasks does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Your tasks

The tasks you enter are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Network access

The app does not connect to the internet.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device by iOS. Your task names are used only to show the reminder on your own phone.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
